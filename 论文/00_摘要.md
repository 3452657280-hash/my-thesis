# 摘要

设备故障排查依赖维修人员的个人经验，而企业积累的大量维修记录以口语化短文本形式分散在表格中，难以检索和复用；通用大语言模型缺少企业设备知识，直接用于诊断容易产生与事实不符的内容。针对这些问题，本文以某汽车零部件企业机加工车间2023—2025年的15319条维修记录为基础，设计并实现了一个基于知识图谱增强大模型的产线故障诊断系统。

首先，设计了包含区域、设备、部件、故障现象、原因和处理措施六类实体的故障本体，采用大模型抽取、规则校验、专家复核与实体规范化相结合的方式，由2023年的维修记录构建了包含6993个实体节点的故障知识图谱，每条关系保留来源记录编号以便溯源。其次，提出了统一检索方法，基于实体词典识别问句中的设备，以二元字组字面覆盖度与BGE-M3向量相似度加权打分，一次检索得到的结果同时用于知识图谱子图、相似记录和决策树。最后，将知识图谱链路与相似记录组织成证据，由大模型生成按原因分支、先易后难的排查决策树，大模型不可用时自动改用规则生成。

实验表明：与精确匹配相比，图谱子图检索的空结果率由50.3%降至7.3%；相似记录检索的Hit@5达到95.8%；在100条留一法测试问句上，无检索证据时大模型的根因命中率仅为2%，同时使用知识图谱和相似记录时达到46%，决策树质量评分（5分制）平均3.86分，显著高于规则方法和无证据的大模型；人工抽检显示六元组整条抽取准确率为32.7%，原因字段是主要短板；在笔记本CPU上检索阶段约2秒完成，无网络时整个诊断约2秒给出结果。系统部署在企业内网，为维修人员快速了解同类故障的历史原因和处理方法提供了支持。

**关键词**：知识图谱；大语言模型；检索增强生成；故障诊断；维修记录

# Abstract

Equipment troubleshooting on production lines relies heavily on the personal experience of maintenance staff. Although enterprises have accumulated large numbers of maintenance records, these records are short, colloquial texts scattered across spreadsheets and are hard to search and reuse, while general-purpose large language models (LLMs) lack enterprise-specific equipment knowledge and tend to produce content that is inconsistent with the facts. Based on 15,319 maintenance records collected from 2023 to 2025 in the machining workshop of an automotive parts manufacturer, this thesis designs and implements a production-line fault diagnosis system based on a knowledge-graph-enhanced LLM.

First, a fault ontology with six entity types — area, equipment, component, symptom, cause and action — is designed. By combining LLM-based extraction, rule-based validation, expert review and entity normalization, a fault knowledge graph with 6,993 entity nodes is built from the 2023 records, and every relation keeps the identifier of its source record for traceability. Second, a unified retrieval method is proposed: equipment mentioned in the query is recognized with an entity dictionary, and candidate records are ranked by a weighted combination of IDF-weighted character-bigram coverage and BGE-M3 vector similarity. The results of a single retrieval are shared by the knowledge subgraph, the similar-record list and the decision tree. Finally, the retrieved graph chains and similar records are organized as evidence, from which the LLM generates a troubleshooting decision tree branched by cause and ordered from low to high cost; when the LLM is unavailable, the system automatically falls back to a rule-based decision tree.

Experiments show that, compared with exact matching, the empty-result rate of subgraph retrieval drops from 50.3% to 7.3%; similar-record retrieval reaches a Hit@5 of 95.8%; on 100 leave-one-out test queries, the root-cause hit rate of the LLM is only 2% without retrieved evidence and rises to 46% when both the knowledge graph and similar records are used, and the generated decision trees receive an average quality score of 3.86 out of 5, significantly higher than the rule-based method and the LLM without evidence; a manual check shows that only 32.7% of the extracted six-tuples are fully correct, with the cause field being the main weakness; on a laptop CPU, retrieval takes about two seconds, and the whole diagnosis takes about two seconds when no network is available. Deployed in the enterprise intranet, the system helps maintenance staff quickly learn the historical causes and solutions of similar faults.

**Keywords**: knowledge graph; large language model; retrieval-augmented generation; fault diagnosis; maintenance records
